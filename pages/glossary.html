TermWhat it means, in plain languageWhere it comes up
API A secure digital link that lets two separate software applications automatically send and request data using set rules. At Lerato's: Connecting the Yoco card machine directly to the Point of Sale system so payment amounts transfer automatically without typing. Systems & integration
Audit trail An audit trail keeps a record of actions or changes, making it possible to track them. At Lerato's: It can display who updates a customer's credit limit and the time of the update. Data & information
Backup A backup is a second copy of information that you can use if the first one gets lost or broken. At Lerato's: Having a safe backup can help restore sales and stock information if the laptop stops working. Data & information
Batch versus real-time Batch updates gather records. Send them together at set times. Real-time updates process transactions instantly as they happen. At Lerato's: Real-time updating shows cement stock counts on the screen the moment a bag is scanned at the till whereas batch updating syncs sales totals. Systems & integration
Consent (POPIA) Consent is when a person freely and clearly says what they want after knowing all the details. At Lerato's: Customers shouldn't be signed up for promotional messages just because a box was already checked. POPIA & ethics
CRM Customer Relationship Management software stores client contact information, credit limits and purchase history in one place. At Lerato's: Keeping a profile for local builder Sipho that records his tax number, approved credit limit and preferred delivery addresses. Systems & integration
Data Raw and unprocessed facts Data & information
Data migration The process of exporting, cleaning, reformatting and importing business records into a new software database. At Lerato's: Exporting 500 inventory items from Leratos Excel stock sheet. Importing them into the POS database. Systems & integration
Data silo / islands of data Standalone. Spreadsheets that store information independently without syncing to systems. At Lerato's: The sales till shows a paint tin costs R250 while the inventory sheet lists it at R230 and the owners notebook says R240. Systems & integration
Data subject A data subject is the person who can be identified by personal information. At Lerato's: A customer who has their credit balance and contact information saved is called a data subject. POPIA & ethics
Dead stock Stock that has not sold for a long time At Lerato's: R23 798 of Leratos stock had no sales in twelve months. Data & information
Descriptive analytics Descriptive analytics provides statistical summaries of past transactional performance and answers the question "What happened?" At Lerato's: A daily summary report generated at 5 PM shows that the store sold worth of hardware items today. AI & decisions
Diagnostic analytics Diagnostic analytics gives a look at data finds the root causes and answers the question "Why did it happen?" At Lerato's: | Filtering sales logs reveals why sales dropped on Tuesday showing that the store ran out of cement bags. AI & decisions
ERP Enterprise Resource Planning: a software platform that manages inventory, accounting, sales and HR within one central system. At Lerato's: A single software package running Leratos sales counter, inventory management, supplier ordering and annual tax accounting simultaneously. Systems & integration
Fit-gap analysis Comparing what the shop needs with what a product does, then deciding what to do about each difference At Lerato's: The system handles selling pipe by the metre is a fit, the gap is having to type the length manually. Making it fit
Gross margin What is left of the selling price after paying what the item cost At Lerato's: If a bag of cement costs R90 and sells for R120 the cross margin is R30 or 25%. Data & information
Information The result of data that has been processed. Data & information
Information Officer An Information Officer handles important tasks related to managing information according to POPIA rules. At Lerato's: The Information Officer is responsible for making sure privacy rules are followed and managing requests related to information. POPIA & ethics

Integration Connecting software applications so they exchange data automatically without manual re-entry. At Lerato's: Linkage between the till and accounting software so every cash sale automatically updates income ledgers. Systems & integration

Point of sale (POS) The checkout software and hardware combination used to scan products, record sales, calculate totals and print receipts. At Lerato's: The touch screen computer, barcode scanner, cash drawer and receipt printer located at the payment counter. Systems & integration
POPIA The Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 is a law in South Africa that controls how personal information is handled and kept safe. At Lerato's: This includes customer names, contact information, credit accounts, and shopping history. POPIA & ethics
Predictive analytics Predictive analytics uses transaction trends and statistical patterns to answer "What is likely to happen?" At Lerato's: Predictive analytics looks at spring sales trends and shows that demand for lawn paint will grow by 30% this month. AI & decisions
Prescriptive analytics Prescriptive analytics provides automated system recommendations or execution scripts that answer "What action should we take?" At Lerato's: | Prescriptive analytics automatically drafts a purchase order for 40 paint tins, based on projected demand. AI & decisions
Processing (POPIA) Processing involves gathering, saving, altering, using or distributing personal information. At Lerato's: When a customer's phone number is written down in an account book, it counts as processing. POPIA & ethics
Progressive disclosure Showing only what is relevant now and revealing more when it becomes relevant At Lerato's: A cash customer is not asked for a credit limit. Making it fit
Provenance Provenance explains where information comes from and how it was gathered or made. At Lerato's: It could find the original bill or inventory list that supports a recorded transaction. Data & information
Record versus row team writes one or two plain sentences, plus this shop's own example Data & information
Referential integrity Referential integrity ensures that connections between related records stay correct. At Lerato's: An invoice should be linked to a customer that is already in the system, not to a nonexistent customer ID. Data & information
Responsible party The person or group in charge decides the reasons and methods for handling personal information. At Lerato's: The shop is in charge of deciding what customer information to collect and how it will be used. POPIA & ethics
Retention Retention means holding onto information for a while. At Lerato's: necessary tax records should be kept for the required time period. However, personal information that isn't needed shouldn't be stored forever. POPIA & ethics
Schema team writes one or two plain sentences, plus this shop's own example Data & information
Shrinkage Stock that disappears between buying and selling it through theft, damage, or recording mistakes. At Lerato's: R3 616 written off or lost to unexplained stock take differences. Data & information
Single source of truth A data management practice where every operational metric or record has one location in the system. At Lerato's: The selling price of a box of nails is stored in the central master product table so that the till, website and quotes all use the price. Systems & integration
Stock turn How many times in a year the shop sells and replaces its average stock At Lerato's: Pipe that sells every week turns quicky and an item that is rarely bought is stuck on the shelf. Data & information
Structured decision Structured decision is a choice governed by objective rules and complete information that can be easily automated. At Lerato's: Calculating the 15% African VAT amount to add to a retail transaction total. AI & decisions
Tax invoice The document a VAT-registered seller gives for a sale, with the details the VAT Act requires At Lerato's: The invoice the system produces for a contractor with the required details on it. Data & information
Total cost of ownership Everything a system costs over its whole life, not just the purchase price. At Lerato's: Lerato's year one is R37 330 before card fees, plus the slower first two weeks and the cost of leaving later. Roadmap

Unstructured decision Unstructured decision involves choices that need human intuition, relational context and judgment not preset software rules. At Lerato's: Deciding whether to sponsor a community soccer club or renegotiate trade terms, with a longstanding supplier. AI & decisions

Vendor lock-in Vendor lock-in is a situation where a software provider makes it difficult or expensive to export company data in order to switch to a competitor. At Lerato's: A POS provider stores Leratos three-year sales history in an encrypted file format that cannot be exported to Excel without paying cancellation penalties. Systems & integration
