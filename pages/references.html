Reference list

Team writes this author: Oratile Nare

Compile the reference list in your required citation style

Cover, in your own words:

  • Use the citation style your department requires. Be consistent — a mixed-style list reads as careless.
  • For legislation, cite the Act properly, including the Act number and year.
  • For vendor pricing, include the URL and the date you accessed it. Prices change; an undated price is unverifiable.
  • For the textbook, include the edition — section numbers and page references differ between editions.
  • Cross-check: every claim in the table above should appear here, and nothing here should be uncited in the body text.

Replace this card with the finished list

Keep it alphabetical, or grouped by type (legislation, books, web sources) with each group alphabetical. Either is defensible; mixing them is not.

A note on the demonstration data

Every person, business, supplier, customer and transaction in the live demonstration is invented for this project. The twelve months of trading is produced by a seeded generator in data/seed.js, which means the same figures appear every time the resource is opened. No real personal information is used anywhere in this resource, and nothing in it is drawn from a real business's records.

Where the demonstration states a quantity — R23 798 of dead stock, 38.3% gross margin, 20.9% customer concentration — that figure is calculated from the invented data set and is presented as an illustration of what such a system would surface, not as a claim about any real South African business.
